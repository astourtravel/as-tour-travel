COMMON QUESTIONS

GOOD TO KNOW

Do I have to pay any advance online?

No online advance payment is required to send a booking request. Confirm the booking and payment terms with our team directly.

Are toll, parking and taxes included in the fare?

These depend on the route and trip plan. Our team will clarify toll, parking, taxes and any applicable night allowance when confirming your fare.

Is night travel allowed?

Yes, you can enquire about night travel through our 24×7 helpline. Vehicle and driver availability will be confirmed on call.

Which areas do you serve?

We serve Lucknow, Kanpur, Agra, Varanasi, Prayagraj, Ayodhya, Noida, Mathura and Delhi NCR, subject to availability.

Do you also help with visa, passport and hotels?

We can assist with hotel booking enquiries. Contact our team to confirm which additional travel services are currently available.
